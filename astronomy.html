A LITTLE COSMOS

Know your sky

01 / THE MOON

One face, many phases

The Moon doesn't make its own light. We see different portions of its sunlit half as it orbits Earth.

02 / THE SUN

Our nearest star

Sunlight takes about 8 minutes and 20 seconds to travel the roughly 150 million kilometres to Earth.

03 / DEEP SPACE

Looking back in time

Light from distant stars and galaxies can take years, thousands of years or much longer to reach us.
